Functional Cluster Production Core Modules & System Capabilities
🧠 Intelligent AI & Analytics • AI Academic Risk Predictor: Early dropout detection and GPA forecasting using ML regression/classification.
• AI Voice & Chatbot Assistant: Independent conversational voice/text assistant for queries and parent alerts.

📐 Methodology & System Architecture

The ecosystem enforces a Decoupled Tiered Microservices Architecture where the Frontend communicates exclusively with the Core ERP Backend, while AI and Voice backends operate independently sharing a secured data infrastructure.

SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & MULTI-TIER COMMUNICATION WORKFLOW
Client Tier (Frontend) Next.js 16 App Router, React 19, Tailwind CSS, Lucide Icons, Client Route Guards (Role-specific portals).
Central Hub (Core ERP) Node.js / Express / TypeScript: Central API Gateway, JWT Authentication, Dynamic RBAC, Multi-Tenant Interceptor.
AI Backend Tier Python / FastAPI: Scikit-Learn, PyTorch/Transformers for student risk forecasting and predictive models.
Voice & Assistant Tier Python / FastAPI / WebSockets: Speech-to-Text (STT), Text-to-Speech (TTS), and Natural Language Chat Query Processing.
Shared Data Tier PostgreSQL 16 (Row-Level Security & pgvector), Redis (Cache & Session Blacklist), MinIO/S3 (Object Storage).
📊 Research & Business Perspective

🔬 5.1 Research Perspective

  • Predictive Student Analytics: Utilizing supervised ML classifiers (Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, LSTMs) to identify at-risk students 6–8 weeks before examinations using attendance trajectories and LMS engagement.
  • Conversational Voice Interactivity: Domain-adapted lightweight LLMs and automated speech engines facilitating localized multilingual voice communication for parents with diverse literacy levels.
  • Algorithmic Fairness & Privacy: Continuous drift monitoring, explainable AI (XAI), and zero-data-leakage architecture guaranteeing compliance with educational privacy standards (GDPR / FERPA).

💼 5.2 Business & SaaS Perspective

  • Subscription Multi-Tenant SaaS Model: Tiered B2B subscription pricing (Basic, Standard, Enterprise) per active student annually, generating high-margin predictable Recurring Revenue (ARR).
  • Institutional Market Scalability: Immense market readiness across primary, secondary, and higher education seeking to replace brittle legacy desktop solutions with automated cloud ERPs.
  • Ecosystem Integration & Global Reach: White-label capability, localized multi-currency payment gateways (bKash, Nagad, Stripe, SSLCommerz), and LMS interoperability (Moodle, Canvas, Google Classroom).
  • Ancillary Revenue Channels: Premium monetization via specialized AI predictive add-ons, enterprise custom form builders, and transactional SMS/Voice communication margins.
